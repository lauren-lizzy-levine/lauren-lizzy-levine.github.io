## About

I am a Computational Linguistics researcher, and I currently work as a Senior Generative AI Engineer at [The MITRE Corporation](https://www.mitre.org/), where I work on a variety of projects involving AI, natural language processing, and data analysis. 

I hold a Ph.D. in Computational Linguistics from the Georgetown University [Department of Linguistics](https://linguistics.georgetown.edu/). I was advised by [Amir Zeldes](https://gucorpling.org/amir/), and I was a member of the [Corpling Lab](https://gucorpling.org/corpling/), [PICoL](https://picol-georgetown.github.io/), and [GUCL: Computational Linguistics @ Georgetown](https://gucl.georgetown.edu/). I received my B.A. with a triple major in Linguistics, Computer Science, and Asian Studies, and a minor in Cognitive Science from Cornell University in May 2018, and I received my M.S. in Computational Linguistics from Georgetown University in December 2024. 

## Research Interests

My academic research focuses on various types of **discourse phenomena**, including bridging anaphora and computational discourse frameworks. I am also broadly interested in **corpus linguistics** (including corpus construction and linguistic annotation) and **computational semantics**.
